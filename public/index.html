Install in Stremio

Enter your TMDB key to generate the add-on link.

Your keys are stored inside the add-on link so Stremio can use them. Don't share the link publicly. This site doesn't save your keys: it passes them on to TMDB (and your poster provider) to fetch titles, but hosting logs may record the addresses requested, which include your link.
