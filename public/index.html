Movies you can rent or buy right now.

A Stremio catalog that hides anything still stuck in theaters. Add your keys, pick a region, and install.

Free at themoviedb.org/settings/api.

Prefilled with the free key t0-free-rpdb, which only covers popular titles (others show plain posters). For full coverage, replace it with your own key from ratingposterdb.com.

Open your provider's configurator and copy its poster URL. Replace the movie ID in it with {imdb_id} (or {tmdb_id} if it wants a TMDB ID). Must start with https://.

Providers: ERDB, XRDB, Posters+, Pictorium. Use the Preview button to check that the posters load.

Install in Stremio

Enter your TMDB key to generate the add-on link.

Your keys are stored inside the add-on link so Stremio can use them. Don't share the link publicly.

Preview

Press "Preview catalog" to see what your settings return.
